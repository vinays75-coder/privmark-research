Title: PrivMark®: A Standardized Privacy Disclosure Framework for AI Systems.

The central contribution is an evidence-backed privacy communication framework,
distinct from engineering privacy safeguards. It describes eleven disclosure
dimensions and interpretable, comparable, auditable profiles for non-experts.
The current local-model experiments demonstrate a limited part of feasibility;
they do not validate all dimensions, stakeholder usability, or formal
standardization. Distinguish proposed contributions from demonstrated findings.
